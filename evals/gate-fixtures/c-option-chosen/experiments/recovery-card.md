# Experiment card — recovery by hand (Wizard of Oz)

Trying to prove: teams that can resume a failed import finish setup the same day.
Type: evaluative
We believe that … resuming where the import stopped gets teams to a working workspace the same day.
To verify that … support restores the partial import manually for the next 10 affected workspaces (2 weeks).
Built: a "Resume import" button that opens a support ticket; support restores by hand within 2 hours.
Measured: same-day setup completion; 7-day return.
Expected: ≥ 6 of 10 complete same day.
Would disprove: ≤ 3 of 10 complete same day.
Found out that: 8 of 10 completed same day; 7 returned within 7 days; support log shows the mapping step is where 6 of the 8 failures originated.
Decision: persevere → solution-options.
