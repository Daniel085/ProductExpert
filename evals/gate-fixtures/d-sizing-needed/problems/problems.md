# Problems — Fixture D

Goal reading: acquisition flip (goal = self-serve ARR; the small-team segment is new to us, so breadth counts on the business side).

## Case: trusted first import for small teams

**Step 1 — Problem statement.** When my team of five signs up and I bring our existing data in for the first time, I want to get to a trusted first import without asking anyone for help, so I can start using the product with my team the same day.

**Step 2 — Evidence**

| Source | Type | Confidence (1–5) | Findings |
|--------|------|------------------|----------|
| 9 interviews with small-team admins, Oct (S1–S9) | Qualitative | 4 | 6 of 9 abandoned or re-did a first import; 5 of 9 import monthly or per report cycle, 3 once at setup; five named what they pay today for workaround tools ($1,200–3,000 / yr) |
| Self-serve trial funnel, last 90 days | Quantitative | 5 | 34% of trials from 10–249-employee companies drop at the import step; completers' 30-day retention 19 pts higher |
| Support tickets from small-team trials | Operational | 3 | 88 tickets / quarter from 47 distinct trials, "import" the top tag |

**Step 3 — Patterns.** Three source types converge. One conflict: interviews suggest monthly use, but we have no usage data past the trial — carried as ledger row A2. No gap by type.

**Step 4 — Verdict: Yes.** ≥ 2 types converge; funnel row at 5; statement passes the job-story tells.

Customer Signal: pain 4. Business Alignment: 3 — breadth is the business criterion under the acquisition flip, and **breadth is unknown**: nobody has counted the small-team market we don't serve.

## Segments (ICP tiers, defined by behaviour)

| Tier | Rule | Evidence |
|------|------|----------|
| T1 — monthly importers | small teams that import structured data at least monthly after setup | 5 of 9 interviewees; frequency unconfirmed by usage data (A2) |
| T2 — setup-only and occasional importers | small teams that import at setup and then less than monthly, including never again | 3 of 9 interviewees said once at setup; the "every report cycle" answers in T1 may belong here if the cycle is quarterly (A2) |
| Out of scope | companies under 10 or over 249 employees; regulated sub-industries needing SOC 2 before trial | capability clause: no SOC 2 on the self-serve plan |
