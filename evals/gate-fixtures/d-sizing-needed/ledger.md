# Assumption Ledger — Fixture D

**Riskiest assumption (exactly one):** A3 — the US small-team market for self-serve data import is large enough to justify a dedicated plan (at least $10M ARR reachable within three years).
**Its cheapest test:** a bottom-up sizing from official firm counts × the interview-evidenced attach rate × the price small teams already pay, reconciled against a top-down figure.
