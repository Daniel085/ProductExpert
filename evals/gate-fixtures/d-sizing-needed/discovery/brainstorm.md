# Brainstorm — self-serve import for small teams

## Framing (job story)

When my team of five signs up and I bring our existing data in for the first time, I want to get to a trusted first import without asking anyone for help, so I can start using the product with my team the same day.

Scope: US companies with 10–249 employees in information and professional-services industries (NAICS 51 and 54), on the self-serve plan, 2026. Today the product serves mid-market and enterprise accounts through onboarding specialists; small teams are not served.

## Options considered
1. A guided self-serve import flow  2. Import templates per source system  3. A concierge first import by support (doesn't scale; a learning test)  4. Remove import; start from templates  5. Partner integrations that sync instead of import  6. (inversion) Accept any file and fix mapping later
Set aside: 4 (removes the job), 6 (feasibility unknown).

## Landscape scan (BACKGROUND)
| Alternative | How | Price | Falls short |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spreadsheet + manual re-entry | the workaround most small teams use today | time | errors, no history |
| Competitor X self-serve plan | CSV import with a mapping wizard | $1,200–2,000 / yr | restarts on failure |
| Competitor Y | integrations only, no CSV | $3,000+ / yr | too costly for teams under 20 |

## Routing
Problem has interview, funnel and ticket evidence → problem-selection. The business question underneath — is this market big enough for a dedicated plan — needs a sizing, not a scan.
