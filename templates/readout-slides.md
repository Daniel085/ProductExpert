## How to use this outline

- **One idea a slide.** The title is the finding as a sentence, not a
  topic ("People leave over price, not the data move", not "Switching").
- **Every number comes from the readout's *Figures* table.** If a figure
  is not there, it does not go on a slide.
- **Caveats live in the speaker notes**, one or two sentences: who the
  source leaves out, what is an estimate, what rests on one thread.
- **What is not validated is said on slide 2 and again on the last
  slide.** A room remembers the first and last things it sees.
- **The decision asked of the room has a slide of its own.**
- **Before presenting, export the deck once in the tool that will show
  it and look at every page.** A layout that is right while editing can
  break on export; large margins, layered panels and decorative shapes
  are where it breaks first.
- Twelve to fourteen slides. If it needs more, the readout is two
  readouts.

## The outline

| # | Slide | What goes on it | Speaker note |
|---|-------|-----------------|--------------|
| 1 | Cover | The initiative as a question or a place ("Where the unmet needs are"), the date, "research readout" | That this is evidence so far, and how much of it is interviews |
| 2 | The answer | Two sentences: what is and is not validated, and the one place to look next | The three points to leave the room with |
| 3 | What we did | Four or five counts from the *Figures* table, **including the number of interviews, even when it is zero** | What each kind of source is |
| 4 | What we arrived with, tested | The claims table: each claim, what the evidence shows, the verdict as problem-selection wrote it | What "restated" means for each restated claim |
| 5 | What came up instead | The top problems the starting idea did not name, three or four, with whose problem each is | The counter-evidence for each |
| 6 | The sharpest problem, in a customer's words | One short quote, who said it and when, two or three facts beside it, and the counter-example | Where the quote is from; that it is one voice |
| 7 | Who already serves this | Provider groups, who is in each, what it means for us | What "nobody found" rests on |
| 8 | Where we would fit | The options as the artifacts have them (complement, replace, leave alone; or the option cards) | Consequences for anything already built |
| 9 | A hypothesis we checked | Only if `hypothesis` ran: the claim, the count, who the source leaves out | The denominator |
| 10 | The size | The sizing's middle figures with ranges, and its tier | Which leaf moves it most |
| 11 | The case, and what is unproven | Who would pay, for what, what it would take; the ledger's top risks in order | What would make the case and what would end it |
| 12 | What happens next | The next steps the gates imply, numbered; **the decision asked of the room** | What is not recommended yet, and why |
| 13 | Appendix: what this rests on | Sources by kind; the limits each agent recorded | Where the working files are |
