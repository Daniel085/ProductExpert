10 Tips to Remember

Talking to Humans

DOs

DON’Ts

Go in prepared. Know your goals and 
questions ahead of time.

Be smart about who you target. Work 
from your hypotheses on market and 
early adopters.

Talk to one person at a time. If you 
bring a note taker, they should remain 
quiet.

Don’t have an endless list of questions.

Don’t take a shotgun approach, talking 
to anyone with a pulse.

Don’t do focus groups.

Prepare yourself to hear things you 
don’t want to hear.

Don’t let your excitement and optimism 
bias what you hear.

Get stories on past behavior.

Don’t ask people to speculate (i.e. 
“would you pay for X?”)

Ask for advice. 

Don’t pitch unless you actually try to 
close for real money.

Listen. 95% of the conversation should 
be them talking.

Don’t talk so much, and don’t be afraid 
of silences. Let them think.

Follow your nose and drill down when 
something of interest comes up.

Don’t feel like you have to rigidly stick 
to a script.

At the end of the interview, ask for 
introductions to more people to speak 
with.

Don’t leave empty handed if you can 
help it.
