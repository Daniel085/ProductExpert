# Source materials — Customer Interviews

The official *Talking to Humans* companion materials are already here
(`TalkingToHumans-*.pdf`, with machine-readable text under `extracted/`). Add
anything else that should ground the agent:

- The free **Talking to Humans** PDF / ebook
- The companion **worksheet**
- Your own notes, highlights, or slides
- Related frameworks (Customer Development, The Mom Test, etc.)

Supported formats the agent can read directly: `.md`, `.txt`, `.pdf`.

When you add something here, fold the specifics into
[`../talking-to-humans.md`](../talking-to-humans.md) (the agent reads that file)
and cite the source under its **Sources & materials** section.
