(`TalkingToHumans-*.pdf`, with machine-readable text under `extracted/`),
as is Melissa Perri's LinkedIn post on David Bland's **Product Death
Cycle** and its AI-era version
(`MelissaPerri-ProductDeathCycle-AIVersion-LinkedIn.pdf` →
`extracted/product-death-cycle-ai-version.txt`) — the anti-pattern
principle 29 and the "agent output is never customer evidence" rule are
drawn from it. Add anything else that should ground the agent:

- The free **Talking to Humans** PDF / ebook
- The companion **worksheet**
- Your own notes, highlights, or slides
- Related frameworks (Customer Development, The Mom Test, etc.)

Supported formats the agent can read directly: `.md`, `.txt`, `.pdf`.

When you add something here, fold the specifics into
[`../talking-to-humans.md`](../talking-to-humans.md) (the agent reads that file)
and cite the source under its **Sources & materials** section.
