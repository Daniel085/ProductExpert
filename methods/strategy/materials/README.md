Also referenced but **not** attached here:

- **HubSpot**, *"Why it's Time to Replace your Funnel with a Flywheel"*
  (hubspot.com/flywheel) — attract · engage · delight; force and
  friction. Read; the vocabulary is already covered by the North Star
  tree's inputs.
- **Cameron Deatsch**, *"10 Lessons on using the Flywheel Effect to
  Grow Your Business"*, Inside Atlassian, 31 Aug 2021 — the enterprise
  trap and "human interaction is a bug" inform two anti-patterns; the
  go-to-market lessons wait for the positioning and pricing agents.

- **Melissa Perri**, *Escaping the Build Trap* (O'Reilly, 2018) — the
  strategy chapters (what strategy is; strategic gaps; the strategic
  framework of vision, strategic intents, product initiatives and
  options; the Product Kata as the process that runs it). A published
  book, distilled in paraphrase in
  [`../product-strategy.md`](../product-strategy.md); nothing quoted.
  Her 2015 blog post on the Product Kata is preserved at
  `methods/lean-experiments/materials/MelissaPerri-TheProductKata.pdf`;
  her 2016 post on product strategy is preserved here (above).
- **Stephen Bungay**, *The Art of Action* (Nicholas Brealey, 2011) —
  the three strategic gaps (knowledge, alignment, effects) and the
  definition of strategy as a deployable decision-making framework that
  Perri adopts. Cited, not reproduced.
- **Mike Rother**, *Toyota Kata* (McGraw-Hill, 2009) and *The Toyota
  Kata Practice Guide* (McGraw-Hill, 2017) — the books the pages above
  summarize; the Practice Guide holds the Starter Kata instructions the
  pages point to.
- The four-step **Product Kata** summary (understand the direction ·
  analyze the current state · set the next goal · execute or deploy) in
  the method doc is **Daniel O'Rorke's**, after Perri.
